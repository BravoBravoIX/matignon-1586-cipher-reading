# v0.5.1

## Abstract

First reading of the whole cipher despatch (Matignon Cipher-3, autumn 1586), near-complete: every page read; 5 short stretches and 5 rare code signs open, all listed. Key: S. Tomokiyo's reconstruction, with values checked on letters deciphered at the time.

## Results in brief

- Every page of BnF fr. 15573 ff. 379r–381v is read. On each page 0.908–0.950 of the signs fall inside French words. The known-answer page, read and decoded the same way, scores 0.907, and the same signs shuffled score 0.59–0.70.
- 115 items remain open. 110 are explained (doubtful words, damage, code signs, reader doubt); 5 are short runs of clear signs not yet read. Five rare code signs are still unknown.
- The reading names the King of Navarre, "your sister" at Agen, Chastillon, the artillery brought out of Navarrenx, Marmande and "fifteen to sixteen thousand crowns".
